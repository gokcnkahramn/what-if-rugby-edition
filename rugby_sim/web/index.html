What If Rugby Edition

Click-driven squad builder. Pick a pool, click a position, click a player - no typing required.

Classic Matches

Pick a real, famous (or surprising) match below. You draft freely from the exact real player pool each side actually had that day - never locked to the real historical XV - then play it live and see how you measure up against the real story.

Season mode

Pick a union and a real calendar year - this union's actual real tournaments that year (whichever of Six Nations / Rugby Championship / Autumn Nations Series / a Rugby World Cup year it really played in) are chained into one season. Draft your own matchday-23 for this union from its widest real pool, then play through every block live/pausable for your own matches while every other nation auto-sims unattended - no manual "play next block" needed. Injuries/suspensions/condition and the "Team form / rankings" ratings all carry over between blocks, not reset per tournament.

Draft mode - build the shared pool

Add any number of union + season combinations (e.g. "Ireland 2026" AND "Ireland 2025" at once - every real player across both seasons joins the shared pool, no duplicates even if the same person appears in both), optionally include the Legends (Hall of Fame) roster, then start the draft. Both sides will pick from this exact combined pool.
